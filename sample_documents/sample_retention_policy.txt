DATA RETENTION POLICY — ACME DIGITAL SERVICES

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines retention periods for personal data processed by Acme Digital Services.

2. RETENTION SCHEDULES
• Customer account data: Retained for active account period
• Transaction records: Retained for 7 years (legal requirement)
• Marketing data: Retained until opt-out
• Log files: Retained for 1 year
