VENDOR PROCESSING AGREEMENT — ACME DIGITAL SERVICES

This agreement is between Acme Digital Services and CloudHost Pro.

1. DATA PROCESSING
CloudHost Pro will process customer personal data on behalf of Acme Digital Services.

2. DATA CATEGORIES
• Customer contact information
• Transaction data
• Analytics data

3. SECURITY MEASURES
CloudHost Pro agrees to implement appropriate technical and organizational measures.

4. SUBPROCESSORS
CloudHost Pro may engage subprocessors with prior notice.

5. CROSS-BORDER TRANSFERS
Data may be transferred to data centers in the United States and Singapore.

6. AUDIT RIGHTS
Acme Digital Services reserves the right to audit CloudHost Pro's compliance.

7. BREACH NOTIFICATION
CloudHost Pro will notify Acme within 48 hours of discovering a breach.
